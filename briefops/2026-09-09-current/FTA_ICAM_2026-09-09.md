## U1 concept
**Accessible Transit Signal Layer**

OmniSign + SIGNAL normalize ASL/AAC/speech/text intent for vehicle identification,
stop announcements, accessible-space location, next-stop requests, and boarding assistance.

Sentinel keeps payment, NEMT/health data, dispatch, and consequential effects behind separate authority.

## Practical eligibility note
FTA strongly recommends organizations that are not existing direct/designated recipients
partner with a current recipient. This artifact therefore recommends a lead transit agency/
eligible nonprofit/recipient partner rather than claiming Unmute1AI can submit alone.
