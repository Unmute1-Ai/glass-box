# WHO / ITU / WIPO GI-AI4H — U1 Opportunity Brief

## Signal
The third Global Initiative on AI for Health meeting is scheduled for September 16–18, 2026 in Hangzhou. The initiative is accepting implemented AI-for-health case studies and participation in a privacy-preserving benchmarking challenge. The challenge evaluates submitted models without training them on the clinical data, and WHO explicitly states that challenge results are not regulatory approval, certification, or endorsement.

## Unmute1AI fit
The strongest U1 angle is evaluation infrastructure rather than clinical decision authority:
- Sentinel can provide principal-bound authorization and immutable evidence receipts around model evaluation;
- OmniSign / SIGNAL can contribute accessibility and communication evaluation dimensions;
- AnnealMesh can be used as an evaluation/orchestration layer while remaining authority-neutral;
- model and component provenance must verify before admission;
- benchmark or inspector results remain advisory evidence only.

## Safety boundary
This artifact is planning material only. It does not submit a model, access clinical records, write to a health system, make a diagnosis, or treat a benchmark result as regulatory approval.
