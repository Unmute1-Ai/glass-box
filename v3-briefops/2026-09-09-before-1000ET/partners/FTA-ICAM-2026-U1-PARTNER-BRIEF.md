# FTA ICAM Pilot — Unmute1AI Partner Brief

**Deadline:** September 9, 2026
**Program:** Innovative Coordinated Access and Mobility (ICAM) Pilot Program
**Total funding:** approximately $11.96M
**Eligible activities include:** technology demonstrations/deployment and accessibility.

## Proposed U1 role
Unmute1AI can serve as an accessibility technology partner around OmniSign + SIGNAL for:
- multimodal rider communication across ASL, speech, text, captions and AAC;
- accessible trip/vehicle/stop information;
- local-first processing where feasible;
- consent-bound transitions into transportation, identity, payment, or health-adjacent systems;
- deterministic Sentinel receipts for consequential agent actions.

## Safety / authority boundary
This document does not authorize submission, spending, transportation dispatch, payment,
health-system access, physical actuation, or official emergency messaging.

## Immediate traction action
Use this as the one-page technical capability insert for outreach to an eligible transit agency,
nonprofit, state/local government, or transportation provider seeking an accessibility technology partner.
