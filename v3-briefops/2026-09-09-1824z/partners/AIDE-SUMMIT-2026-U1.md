# AIDE Summit 2026 — Unmute1AI Partnership Brief

## Signal
The A.I. for Disasters + Emergencies (AIDE) Summit is convening public-sector, civil-society,
technology and emergency-management leaders in Austin on September 9, 2026.

## U1 fit
Unmute1AI can contribute an accessibility-and-authority layer for emergency AI systems:

- ASL, captions, speech, text, AAC, haptics and accessible visual description;
- local-first fallback when connectivity is impaired;
- clear separation between advisory AI output and authenticated official alerts;
- explicit cross-domain consent for sensitive transitions;
- scoped single-use credentials for consequential effects;
- deterministic Sentinel receipts for who authorized what.

## Partnership posture
Position U1 as an interoperability/evaluation partner for accessible emergency communication,
not as an issuer of emergency alerts. A pilot should connect to an authenticated public-authority
integration before any message can be represented as official.

## Safety boundary
This artifact creates no official alert, emergency dispatch, physical actuation, health-system write,
financial transaction, procurement commitment or grant submission.
