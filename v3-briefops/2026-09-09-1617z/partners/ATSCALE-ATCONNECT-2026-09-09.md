# ATscale / ATconnect Partnership Signal — 2026-09-09

ATscale's ATconnect community is marking its first year with a global webinar focused on how the platform is being used across the assistive-technology ecosystem and where collaboration should go next.

## Unmute1AI fit

OmniSign, SIGNAL and PRIMER can be positioned as accessibility-native multimodal infrastructure rather than another single-purpose assistive app:

- ASL, speech, captions, text, AAC, haptics and accessible visual description;
- local-first inference where practical;
- modality adapters that never inherit principal authority;
- explicit consent for sensitive domain transitions;
- verifiable component/model provenance and deterministic action receipts.

## Recommended partnership posture

Use this as an ecosystem-introduction target, with a request to contribute technical evaluation, interoperability testing or a small accessibility pilot rather than arriving with a predetermined product sale.

## Safety boundary

This artifact does not enroll users, enter contracts, spend funds, submit grants, access health systems, actuate devices, or represent emergency information as official.
