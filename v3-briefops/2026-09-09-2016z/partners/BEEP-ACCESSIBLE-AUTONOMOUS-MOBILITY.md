# Beep Autonomous Mobility — U1 Accessibility Pilot Brief

## Signal
Beep announced a Series B led by Mobileye to expand AI-driven autonomous mobility networks and its AutonomOS orchestration platform.

## U1 fit
OmniSign + SIGNAL can be positioned as the accessible communication and consent layer around autonomous transit operations:
- ASL, speech, captions, text, AAC, haptics and accessible visual description;
- accessible rider instructions and disruption messaging;
- local-first fallback where practical;
- explicit consent for sensitive cross-domain transitions;
- separation of rider communication from vehicle actuation authority;
- Sentinel receipts for consequential integrations.

## Safety boundary
U1 may interpret, translate, display, propose or simulate. It must not issue vehicle actuation, dispatch, payment, health-system writes or official emergency alerts without the separately authenticated authority integration required for that domain and a fresh action-bound single-use credential.

## Partnership posture
Approach as an accessibility/interoperability evaluation partner for public-sector autonomous mobility pilots, not as an autonomous-driving control supplier.
