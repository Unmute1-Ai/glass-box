# Young Futures Groundwork — PRIMER Opportunity Brief

## Signal
Young Futures launched Groundwork, a $15 million, three-year U.S.-U.K. initiative focused on helping young people thrive as AI reshapes learning and decision-making. The first funding challenge, The Unwritten Challenge, opens September 23, 2026.

## PRIMER fit
PRIMER can position around accessibility-native AI learning:
- ASL, captions, speech, AAC, text, haptics and accessible visual interaction;
- age-appropriate consent and principal-bound authority;
- local-first or privacy-sensitive deployment options;
- transparent model/component provenance;
- evidence that accessibility is part of the learning architecture, not an add-on.

## Recommended artifact
Prepare a concise challenge-fit concept centered on preserving learner agency, communication access and critical reasoning while using AI.

## Safety boundary
This artifact does not submit an application, enroll a student, alter school records, spend funds, or create an educational commitment.
