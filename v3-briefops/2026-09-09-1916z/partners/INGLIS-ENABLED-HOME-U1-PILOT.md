# Inglis Enabled Home Showcase — U1 Pilot Brief

## Signal
Inglis is hosting its Opening Doors Together conference on September 9–10, including an Enabled Home Showcase for technologies that improve independence and quality of life for disabled people and older adults.

## U1 fit
OmniSign + SIGNAL can be positioned as a local-first accessible communication layer for enabled homes:
- ASL, speech, captions, text, AAC and haptics;
- on-device perception where practical;
- explicit consent for sensitive cross-domain transitions;
- simulation/proposal separated from physical actuation;
- authenticated hardware authority plus fresh action-bound single-use credentials for any consequential device effect.

## Partnership posture
Seek evaluation or pilot collaboration, not autonomous home-control authority.

## Safety boundary
This artifact does not actuate any home device, enter a contract, enroll users, perform a health-system write, or issue an official emergency alert.
