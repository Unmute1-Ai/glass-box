# Abilities Expo Phoenix 2026 — OmniSign / SIGNAL Opportunity Brief

## Signal
Abilities Expo Phoenix runs September 11–13, 2026 at WestWorld of Scottsdale, bringing disability-community
users, assistive-technology vendors, service providers, educators, and accessibility professionals together.

## U1 fit
OmniSign and SIGNAL are strong demonstration candidates:
- real-time ASL, captions, speech, text, AAC, haptics, and accessible visual description;
- local-first / edge inference where practical;
- privacy-preserving modality processing;
- modality adapters that never inherit principal authority;
- explicit consent for sensitive domain transitions.

## Recommended use
Treat the expo as user-validation and partnership discovery, not merely marketing. Useful outputs would be:
a concise demo script, accessibility test card, pilot one-pager, and partner outreach list.

## Safety boundary
This artifact does not register for the event, enter a contract, collect user biometrics, actuate devices,
write to health systems, move funds, or issue official emergency alerts.
