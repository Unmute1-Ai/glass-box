## Unmute1AI opportunity

OmniSign / SIGNAL / PRIMER as accessibility-native modality layers.

A practical pilot thesis:
- Gemini or another frontier model handles reasoning and planning;
- Sentinel binds principal, tool, resource, policy, provenance, consent, and effect credentials;
- model substitution produces authority delta = 0;
- accessibility modalities do not inherit effect authority;
- external scanners remain advisory.
