# PRIMER Partner Artifact — Google × Missouri Statewide AI Education

## Signal
Google announced a statewide Missouri partnership providing students, educators and residents with no-cost AI tools, training and professional certificates.

## U1 opportunity
Position PRIMER as an accessibility-native AI tutoring and learning layer for state/district pilots where mainstream AI training programs still need:
- ASL, captions, AAC, speech, text, switch and accessible visual-description interaction;
- local-first or privacy-sensitive deployment options;
- age-appropriate consent and principal-bound authority;
- transparent model/component provenance;
- deterministic evidence for consequential integrations.

## Partnership thesis
PRIMER complements broad AI literacy programs by making access usable for learners who are routinely underserved by default interfaces.

## Safety boundary
This artifact is outreach/planning material only. It does not submit applications, enroll students, modify school records, purchase services, or create official educational commitments.
