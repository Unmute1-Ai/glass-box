## Suggested pitch thesis
Unmute1AI proposes an authority-preserving AI orchestration layer for hybrid classical/quantum and autonomous research systems. The system separates model cognition from deterministic authorization, verifies model/component provenance, requires explicit consent for sensitive domain transitions, and produces cryptographic evidence receipts for consequential actions.
