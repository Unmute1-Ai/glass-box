# UN Assistive Technology Resolution — U1 Partnership Alignment

## Signal
The United Nations General Assembly adopted its first resolution on access to assistive technology in September 2026, recognizing assistive technology as essential to inclusion, sustainable development, and implementation of disability rights.

## Unmute1AI alignment
U1 can map OmniSign, SIGNAL and PRIMER to the resolution's implementation themes:
- multimodal communication access across ASL, speech, captions, text, AAC and haptics;
- affordable local-first / edge deployment where practical;
- interoperability with existing assistive-technology ecosystems;
- privacy-preserving modality handling;
- explicit consent for sensitive cross-domain transitions;
- deterministic authorization and evidence receipts for consequential actions.

## Partnership targets
Use the resolution as a policy-alignment artifact for outreach to ATscale, disability organizations, public-sector accessibility programs, transit/education pilots, and international implementation partners.

## Safety boundary
